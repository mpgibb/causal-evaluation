# Data provenance

Status: synthetic unit-test fixtures only, authored for this starter.

No dataset download or redistribution occurs. Before research begins, record:
- Dataset source, version, retrieval date, and license.
- Unit of analysis, field definitions, missingness, and exclusions.
- Event timestamps and what was available at each decision timestamp.
- Train/validation/test construction and entity overlap controls.
- Synthetic-generating assumptions and external-validity limits, if used.

Domain assumptions: Parallel untreated trends, no anticipation or interference, stable composition. Two periods cannot establish these assumptions.
